Stake

Staked SWIPE earns USDC fees and esSWIPE emissions from every payment after you stake. Payments that happened earlier aren't yours — your starting point is set the moment you stake, which is the whole reason this is fair.

Convert escrow Locks stake

esSWIPE can't be sold or sent. It becomes real SWIPE by vesting over a year — and while it does, an equal amount of your staked SWIPE can't be withdrawn. It's a lock, not a penalty: cancel any time and the unvested part goes back to escrow.

SWIPE minted for a refunded sale is burned, so supply follows net volume rather than gross. Where a merchant had already moved theirs, the shortfall is published as unrecovered rather than quietly ignored. Emissions track fees rather than a clock. A month with no payments emits nothing, because nothing was earned — there is no schedule to front-run and no way for supply to grow without someone having paid for something.
