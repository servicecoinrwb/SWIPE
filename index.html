The case, in one number

A $38,000 job. Two ways to get paid.

Card processors charge a flat percentage no matter the size, which stops making sense somewhere around the third zero.

A card at 2.9% + 30¢

$1,102

Here

$140

In your wallet before the customer puts their phone away.

The fee is charged in brackets, like tax — 2.5% on the first $500, 1% to $5,000, a quarter percent above. Each rate applies only to the slice inside it, so the effective rate falls as the ticket grows and never jumps.

01

Settles instantly

Not tonight, not in two days. The customer signs once and the money is in your wallet in that same transaction — because nothing here holds a balance between them.

02

You earn as you process

One SWIPE minted per dollar of volume, straight to the merchant. Stake it and you take a cut of everyone else's fees — including your own.

03

Nobody owns it

No premine, no treasury, no admin key, no fee switch. One privileged action existed at deployment, it was used once, and it can never be used again.
